Two powerful modules

Built for learning and communication

Each module is purpose-built — one to translate what you sign, one to teach you how to sign it correctly.

Module 1

ISL Translator

Sign words in front of your webcam. The system captures your hand movements in real time, predicts each word using an LSTM model, builds them into a sentence, translates it, and reads it aloud.

  • Real-time hand landmark detection
  • LSTM sequence prediction
  • Hindi, Kannada, English translation
  • Audio output via text-to-speech
Module 2

Smart AI Tutor

Select a word to learn. A ghost hand overlay shows you the correct finger positions. Attempt the sign and get live accuracy feedback. Earn badges when your accuracy crosses 90%.

  • Ghost hand overlay guidance
  • MLP-based accuracy scoring
  • Finger-level feedback
  • Time Attack gamified mode
The pipeline

From gesture to meaning

Every gesture goes through a four-stage pipeline powered by MediaPipe and deep learning models trained on ISL data.

01

Webcam capture

Browser streams your live video feed at 30fps using the webcam API.

02

Landmark extraction

MediaPipe detects 21 hand landmarks per hand — 126 coordinates per frame.

03

Model prediction

Sequences are sent to the Flask backend where LSTM or MLP predicts the sign.

04

Output delivery

Translated text appears on screen with audio playback in your chosen language.
